Your data lives in your browser. That is the whole architecture.

There is no Financial Vault server to breach, subpoena, or shut down. Accounts, documents, expenses, contacts, and notes are written to your browser's IndexedDB and localStorage, and nowhere else.

No account to create or leak

Open the app and start. There is nothing to sign up for, so there is no login database holding your name beside your balances.

AI sees statement text only if you allow it

Without a Groq key, parsing runs entirely on rules in your browser. With one, the call goes straight from your browser to Groq. It never passes through anything of ours.

Backups are yours to keep

One .json export holds everything. Because nothing syncs, that file is your recovery plan, and the app reminds you when it's stale.

Everything a household balance sheet needs, in one place

Five working tabs, plus a continuity plan below. All of them read from the same local data, so a statement you drop in once shows up in your net worth, your budget, and your goals.

Drop in a statement, confirm what it found

Upload a PDF, a CSV, or a photo. The vault reads the institution, account type, balance, APR, and the statement's real as-of date, then asks you to confirm before anything is saved.

Every upload stays stored and viewable, and accounts flag themselves when their statement gets old. For a mortgage or auto loan, add the asset's value so net worth reflects equity, not just the debt.

Install it on any device

It's a full Progressive Web App. Installed, it opens in its own window with its own icon, and a service worker keeps the app shell available without a connection.

Desktop: Chrome or Edge

  1. Open the app
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar, or use the in-app Install App button

iPhone and iPad: Safari

  1. Open the app in Safari
  2. Tap Share
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen

Android: Chrome

  1. Open the app
  2. Tap the in-app Install App button, or Menu, then Install app

Your data is local either way. Install just gives it a home screen icon and an offline shell. Open the app to begin.

What to know before you rely on it

Local-first is a real tradeoff. Here is the fine print, up front.

Back it up yourself

Settings, then Export Complete Backup, writes one .json file. Clear your browser data or lose the device without a backup and the vault is gone. Treat that file like a password manager export.

The PIN is a lock screen, not encryption

Optional PIN lock gates the app on a device. It does not encrypt what's stored in the browser, so use your device's own protections too.

Check what the parser extracts

Statement parsing, AI or rule-based, can make mistakes. The app asks you to confirm figures before saving. Do that every time.

Not financial, legal, or tax advice

Strategy reports and allocation suggestions come from the numbers you entered. They support your record-keeping and are no substitute for a licensed professional.
